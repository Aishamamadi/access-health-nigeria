Finding the right care shouldn't be a guessing game

In northern Nigeria, millions of people bypass PHCs — not because they don't care about their health, but because they don't understand or trust the system. Access Health Nigeria addresses navigation and trust, in Hausa, where people already are.

Why USSD reaches further
USSD works on any phone — no smartphone, no internet, no data required. It is how millions of Nigerians already do mobile banking. It reaches the 54% who are offline and the communities that need this most.

Two-channel strategy
WhatsApp for urban and peri-urban users with smartphones. USSD for rural users without internet. Same navigation logic, two delivery channels — maximising reach across Nigeria's digital divide.
